01 / Choose

Static residential IP, ISP proxy, or rotating residential proxy?

The right Proxy-Seller product depends on whether you need a stable address, rotation, broad website compatibility, mobile-network reputation, or the lowest unit cost. Test a small order against your actual target before scaling.

IPv4 datacenter

Stable, broad compatibility. A practical starting point for general browsing, monitoring, testing, and tasks that do not require a residential ASN.

Choose it when predictable static IPs and HTTP(S)/SOCKS5 support matter more than residential appearance.

IPv6 datacenter

Large address space, lower cost. Useful for high-volume work where the target supports IPv6.

Check target-site IPv6 support first; compatibility varies more than with IPv4.

ISP

Static residential-class addresses. ISP-assigned IPs combine long sessions with stronger consumer-network signals.

Useful for stable logins, regional SaaS access, monitoring, and workflows that need a consistent location.

Residential

Rotating real-user IP pool. Supports country, city, and ISP targeting plus rotating or sticky sessions.

Best suited to public-data collection, price monitoring, ad verification, and research that needs geographic diversity.

Mobile

Carrier-network traffic. Useful when a workflow must be tested from mobile networks or carrier locations.

Usually the most specialised choice. Validate concurrency, rotation, location, and cost before committing.
